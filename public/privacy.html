Mingleverse Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Mingleverse ("we," "us," "our") is a social app that helps people meet new friends through chat and voice conversations. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: email address or your identity from a third-party sign-in provider (Google, Discord), username, and date of birth (used only to confirm you're 18 or older).
  • Profile info: avatar selections, bio, interests, personality tags, and any prompt answers you choose to add.
  • Content: messages you send in matches, hangouts, and chats, and metadata about voice calls (call duration, participants) — we do not record or store the audio of your voice calls.
  • Safety data: reports you file, blocks you place, and the session/account details tied to a report, used to keep the community safe.
  • Region: a coarse region (e.g. country-level) used only to match you with people in the same matching pool. We do not display your location to other users.
  • Device/technical info: basic diagnostic data needed to keep the app running (crash logs, connection info for voice calls).

How We Use Information

  • To operate the core features: matching, chat, voice calls, friendships, and hangouts.
  • To enforce our age requirement (18+) and community safety rules.
  • To review reports, act on abuse, and ban accounts that violate our rules.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of service providers to run Mingleverse:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file storage.
  • LiveKit — real-time voice call infrastructure.
  • Google / Discord — optional sign-in providers, if you choose to use them. We only receive the basic profile information (like your email and display name) needed to create your account.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

Mingleverse is restricted to users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and accounts found to misrepresent their age are banned.

Your Choices

You can block or report any user directly in the app. You can edit or remove most profile information from your Profile screen at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
